Esteemed Guests, and Fellow Seekers of the Divine,

On this auspicious day of Ram Navami, we stand at the confluence of history and divinity. 
We are here to celebrate the sacred festival of Ram Navami.
Our hearts are filled with joy, happiness and devotion.

This is not just the birthday of Lord Ram—it is a celebration of dharma, maryada, and the eternal values that shape our lives.

We know that Lorm Ram has been portrayed as Maryada Puroshottama. From the scriptures, we should learn and imbibe the 3 basic principles.

PCR - Principles - Characteristics - Relevance

Principles
----------
Strength is measured not by power, but by character.
Leadership is defined not by authority, but by responsibility
Victory is won not through ego, but through truth and humility.

Characteristics
---------------
A son who walked into exile with a smile. 
A husband who crossed oceans for love.
A leader who fought for justice not for victory.


Relevance in Life
-----------------
When we are confused/misguided/misdirected, Lord Ram becomes our direction.
When we are weak, Lord Ram becomes our courage.
When we feel alone, Lord Ram becomes our companion.

Ram Navami is not just a festival — it is a reminder that light always returns, no matter how long the night is.

May Lord Ram bless us with courage like his, devotion like Hanuman’s, and purity like Sita Mata’s.
May His light guide us, protect us, and inspire us always.
Jai Shri Ram!
